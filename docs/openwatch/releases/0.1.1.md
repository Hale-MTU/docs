# OpenWatch 0.1.1

Released 2026-11-07.

The first OpenWatch release: the watch and scale code that lived in OpenVitals, as its own app. A Garmin watch syncs its activities, wellness and sleep over Garmin's own Bluetooth protocol; a Wear OS watch records heart rate, steps and floors in the OpenWatch watch app and syncs them on a tap or a schedule; a Xiaomi S400 or S400 Pro scale saves every weigh-in as it happens. Everything is written to Health Connect, where OpenVitals or any other Health Connect app shows it. No account, no cloud, no internet permission.

### Added

- **Garmin watches.** Sync, notification forwarding, alarms, the settings tree, find my watch, music controls, calendar, weather, CoMaps guidance, a GPS ephemeris upload and saved locations. The watch-only measurements stay on the Watch Data screen.
- **Wear OS watches.** The OpenWatch watch app, with tiles for heart rate, steps and floors.
- **Xiaomi S400 and S400 Pro.** Weigh-ins decoded from the scale's broadcast with the key from your Xiaomi account.

The phone app and the Wear OS app share the package name `tech.mmarca.openwatch` and the signing certificate; the watch's versionCode is 2000000000 plus the phone's.

Distribution flow:

- GitHub release with the signed phone APK, signed debug APK, signed Wear OS APK and both signed Android App Bundle assets.
- Google Play: the update follows later. This version goes to internal testing on the phone track and the Wear OS track while the app awaits Google's approval; the production upload comes once it is granted.
