# OpenBridge

OpenBridge is a separate, optional app that reads the data OpenVitals chooses to share and can pass it on to online services. It is the only app in the family with internet access, so OpenVitals itself never needs it. It is early stage: no service is connected yet.

> **Documentation is being written.** These pages are placeholders. Until they are filled in, the [OpenBridge page](https://hale.ngo/en/openbridge/) describes the app and the [repository README](https://github.com/Hale-MTU/OpenBridge#readme) explains how it reads data.
